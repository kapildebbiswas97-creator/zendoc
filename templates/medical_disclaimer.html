Safety boundary

Medical Disclaimer

ZENDOC supports healthcare navigation and continuity; it does not replace licensed clinical judgment.

Use ZENDOC as support, not as a diagnosis

AI guidance, educational material, search results, summaries and wellness features are informational. They should not be treated as a diagnosis, prescription, medication change, treatment order, or confirmation that a provider has accepted or completed care.

Emergencies

ZENDOC does not claim autonomous emergency dispatch. If you believe there is an emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency service or seek urgent in-person care directly.

Medication and treatment

Do not start, stop, substitute, or change the dose of a medicine solely because of ZENDOC AI output. Medication decisions require an appropriately qualified clinician or pharmacist.

Records and provenance

Provider-recorded, user-reported, document-extracted and device-recorded information remain distinct. Appointment completion is operational evidence only and does not prove a diagnosis or treatment outcome.
