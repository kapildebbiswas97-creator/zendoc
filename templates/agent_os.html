Autonomy contract

Power with hard boundaries

Automatic: search, compare, summarize, plan, change search parameters, check availability and prepare/stage reversible work.

User gate: booking, cancellation/rescheduling, orders, paid services, record sharing and permission changes.

Owner gate: promotion of model/prompt/routing candidates and permission-policy changes.

Hard blocked: payment execution/secrets, prescribing, medicine changes, emergency dispatch, arbitrary code/SQL/shell, secret access, self-deployment and safety bypass.

Unknown actions fail closed until explicitly classified and tested.
