Health Community safety policy

ZENDOC Health Community Guidelines

Effective 19 September 2026. These rules apply to posts, stories, comments, media links and other user-generated community content.

1. Keep it health-focused

Community content should relate to healthy food, fitness and movement, sleep and recovery, mental wellbeing, caregiving, preventive health, health technology, or lived care-navigation experience.

2. Community content is not clinical authority

Do not present a community post as a diagnosis, prescription, individualized treatment order, emergency instruction, or verified clinical evidence. Personal experience may be shared as personal experience, not as proof that the same outcome applies to someone else.

3. Medicines and high-risk claims

Do not use Community to sell prescription medicines, tell another person to start/stop/change a medicine or dose, or make unsupported cure claims. Medicine requests belong in the controlled ZENDOC Pharmacy workflow.

4. Safety, abuse and privacy

Do not post threats, harassment, hateful abuse, sexual exploitation, doxxing, another person's private medical record, or content that encourages self-harm or dangerous behavior. Do not upload or link media you do not have the right or consent to share.

5. Commercial content

Sponsored, affiliate or otherwise compensated content must be clearly disclosed. Commercial relationships must not be represented as medical endorsement, and they do not influence ZENDOC clinical or provider ranking.

6. Reporting, blocking and moderation

Users can report content and block accounts. The ZENDOC owner moderation queue can remove reported posts, stories or comments. A single report does not automatically disable an account; account-level action requires owner review.

7. Enforcement

ZENDOC may remove content or restrict community access when necessary to enforce these rules, protect users, comply with law, or preserve platform security. Moderation does not convert remaining user-generated content into verified medical advice.

8. Related policies

Community use is also subject to the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Medical Disclaimer.
