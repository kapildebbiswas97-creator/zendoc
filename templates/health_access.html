eKYC readiness with truthful verification status

Integration required

ZENDOC is prepared for a consent-first identity verification flow, but this competition build does not claim a live Aadhaar, ABHA, DigiLocker, government eKYC, or other identity-provider integration. Until an authorized provider returns verifiable evidence, the user must remain Not verified.

1. Identity profile

Keep ordinary account identity details separate from sensitive government identifiers. Do not collect more information than the verification purpose requires.

2. Explicit consent

Show the verification purpose before any external handoff. Identity consent does not grant medical-record access.

3. Authorized verification

A future approved connector can perform the real eKYC step. ZENDOC must never generate its own “verified” result.

4. Evidence & audit

Store only the minimum returned verification status and provenance needed for audit, subject to retention and privacy rules.

Current status: Not verified / external integration not connected.
