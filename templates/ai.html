Mental wellness & awareness

A visible place to check in with yourself

Awareness

This space is for everyday wellbeing awareness, reflection, and safe next-step guidance. It does not diagnose a mental-health condition and it does not replace a qualified professional.

Notice

Pause and name what you are feeling, what changed recently, and what is making the day harder.

Stabilize

Use simple grounding, sleep, hydration, movement, and social-support steps when they are appropriate for you.

Reach out

If stress is persistent, worsening, or affecting daily life, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional.

Urgent safety

If you may hurt yourself or someone else, or you feel in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted person nearby now.
