Safety Architecture

Clinical boundaries by design

  • Emergency triage first: Emergency keywords are checked and escalated before any generative response.
  • Educational guidance only: AI recommendations never fabricate prescriptions or pretend to replace licensed doctors.
  • Deterministic routing: Recommendations point to real provider profiles, appointments, and records.
🔒

Privacy Architecture

You own your health data

  • Consent-controlled sharing: Providers and specialists only see medical records when explicitly granted.
  • No data monetization: Your personal vitals, reports, and messages are protected with role-based access.
  • Transparent status: Every feature is clearly labeled as working, beta, or prototype.
