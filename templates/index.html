Safety Architecture

Clinical boundaries by design

  • Emergency-first: urgent signals are checked before routine guidance.
  • Educational guidance: AI does not pretend to replace licensed medical care.
  • Truthful actions: no data or integration is never presented as successful real-world execution.
🔒

Privacy Architecture

You stay in control of your health data

  • Consent-controlled sharing: access is governed by explicit permissions.
  • Provenance: ZENDOC keeps source and status context attached to supported records.
  • Transparent status: working, beta, prototype, unknown, and integration-required states are kept distinct.
