Mental wellness & awareness

A visible, private place to check in with yourself

Everyday wellbeing awareness, reflection and safe next-step guidance. ZENDOC does not diagnose a mental-health condition, perform personality scoring, prescribe medicine, or replace a qualified professional.

Non-diagnostic
Urgent safety comes first.
If you may hurt yourself or someone else, or you feel in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted person nearby now. Do not wait for an online response.

Awareness

Four simple steps

Notice

Name what you are feeling, what changed recently and what is making the day harder.

Stabilize

Use appropriate basics such as grounding, sleep, hydration, movement and social support.

Reach out

If stress is persistent, worsening or affecting daily life, consider qualified professional support.

Escalate safely

For immediate danger or risk of harm, use local emergency services and trusted people nearby.
