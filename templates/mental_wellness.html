Awareness

Four simple steps

Notice

Name what you are feeling, what changed recently and what is making the day harder.

Stabilize

Use appropriate basics such as grounding, sleep, hydration, movement and social support.

Reach out

If stress is persistent, worsening or affecting daily life, consider qualified professional support.

Escalate safely

For immediate danger or risk of harm, use local emergency services and trusted people nearby.

Safety-first guidance
