# ZENDOC Google Play Listing Draft

Use this as a safe starting point only after the production domain, Privacy Policy, Data Safety form, Health apps declaration, and release build are verified.

## App name

ZENDOC

## Short description

Connected healthcare navigation, Health Memory, appointments, wellness and bounded AI.

## Full description

ZENDOC helps people organize a connected healthcare journey in one place.

Use ZENDOC to discover healthcare options, manage your personal Health Memory, keep records and measurements organized, request appointments with connected providers, follow care tasks, and access everyday fitness, nutrition and wellness tools.

Key capabilities in the current product include:

- Healthcare discovery with source and verification status kept visible.
- Connected-provider appointment requests where real ZENDOC availability exists.
- Patient-controlled Health Memory, timeline, records and measurements.
- Care Continuity with clinician handoff preparation and evidence provenance.
- Messages and family-care workflows with permission controls.
- Fitness, nutrition and wellness features.
- Bounded ZENDOC AI for guidance, navigation and workflow assistance.
- Account data controls, including in-app account deletion.

### Important healthcare notice

ZENDOC AI guidance is informational and may be incomplete or wrong. ZENDOC does not independently diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medicines, change medication or dosage, or replace a qualified medical professional.

External/public provider listings are discovery references unless the app explicitly shows a connected, verified ZENDOC provider workflow.

ZENDOC does not claim autonomous emergency dispatch. In an emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency service or seek urgent in-person care directly.

### Privacy and control

Health information is handled according to ZENDOC's Privacy Policy and permission/consent controls. Provider-recorded, user-reported, document-extracted and device-recorded information remain provenance-labelled. Prior AI chat is not treated as medical evidence.

Users can initiate account deletion from inside ZENDOC or through the public account-deletion web resource.

## Do not put these claims in the listing unless independently proven

- "AI doctor" as a replacement for physicians.
- Diagnostic accuracy percentages without validated evidence.
- "FDA/CE approved" or other regulatory claims without actual authorization.
- "Runs on Snapdragon NPU" without measured device/Qualcomm evidence.
- Nationwide connected provider/bed/medicine coverage without real integration evidence.
- Guaranteed appointments, stock, insurance approval, treatment outcome, or emergency response.
- Investor/hackathon selection claims that did not actually occur.
